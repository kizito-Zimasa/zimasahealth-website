## 3. OG Image System

OG images are a separate production task from photography. They render at thumbnail scale in WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Twitter. They are not hero crops.

### Recommended approach

Build one template in Figma or Canva with:
- Brand colour background (green `#0A6E38` for most pages; navy `#0D2133` for Payers/B2B pages)
- Zimasa logo top-left (white version)
- Page title: 48–56px, Montserrat Bold, white, centred or left-aligned
- Optional: tagline or category label in smaller type below
- Optional: subtle geometric brand element (lines or shape from §13.1)

Swap only the title and background colour per page. 30–45 minutes produces all 16 page OG images.

### File naming convention

```
/public/images/og-[page-slug].jpg
```

Examples:
```
/public/images/og-home.jpg
/public/images/og-insurers-tpas.jpg
/public/images/og-employers.jpg
/public/images/og-get-started.jpg
/public/images/blog/half-trillion-problem-og.jpg
```

## 5. Sourcing Strategy

### Phase 1 launch (stock imagery)

Use stock photography for hero images. Apply CSS overlays to maintain brand consistency — do not Photoshop every image.

**Recommended sources:**

| Source | Cost | Best for |
|--------|------|---------|
| Unsplash (free) | Free | Supporting lifestyle images, workplace contexts |
| Pexels (free) | Free | African business and wellness contexts |
| Getty Images / iStock | Paid | Higher quality; better Africa filter |
| Shutterstock | Paid | Broadest East Africa library |

**Search terms that work:**
- "Kenya business office" / "Nairobi workplace"
- "East Africa wellness" / "African fitness community"
- "African healthcare professional" (filter to non-clinical settings)
- "African team workplace" / "African colleagues"
- "African woman exercising" / "African man running"

**Filter out:** studio white backgrounds, US/European office settings, obvious stock poses (direct camera handshake, three-people-looking-at-laptop-grinning).

### Phase 2 — Commissioned photography (Q3 2026)

One half-day shoot in Nairobi replaces all stock photos with owned, authentic imagery. Plan for Q3 after go-live.

**Shoot brief for when this is commissioned:**
- 3–4 subjects (mix of gender, age 25–45, professional contexts)
- Locations: modern Nairobi office setting, outdoor/active setting, informal wellness context
- Wardrobe: business casual — not suits, not gym wear only
- Output needed: 30–40 hero-quality images, variety of crops (wide and portrait)
- Rights: full commercial licence, model releases for all subjects
- Estimated cost: KES 40,000–80,000 depending on photographer

## 6. Product / Dashboard Mockup Creation

Dashboard and app mockups need to be built, not sourced. They are the most important product credibility signal on the site.

### What to build for launch (Tier 1 only)

**Payers dashboard mockup** — highest priority on the entire site:
- Show a 30/60/90 day activation view
- Key metrics visible: activation rate %, Active Days count, cohort breakdown
- Mocked data: plausible Kenyan numbers — "47% activation at Day 30 → 61% at Day 60"
- Fictitious org name: e.g. "Savannah Health Scheme — Q1 2026 Programme"
- Never use real client names, real member names, or real health data

**Zimasa app screen** (home page and Zimasa Go sections):
- Steps tracking view showing a weekly streak
- Challenge leaderboard showing a squad of 4–5 with Active Days counts
- Share card design if available

### Mockup tool
Figma (preferred) or any design tool Dalton uses. Export as WebP for site use, JPG for OG use.

## 7. Moodboard Direction

Build three moodboards before final image selection. Share with Founder for approval before sourcing begins.

### Moodboard 1 — B2B / Payers
**Keyword**: structured trust
Visual direction: professional African business contexts; data and reporting feel; modern but not cold; navy and teal tones dominate; a product screenshot is always paired with a human image

### Moodboard 2 — Employers / Teams
**Keyword**: thriving participation
Visual direction: small groups, colleagues, workplaces; energetic but real; green tones; someone checking a phone or leaning over a laptop; a sense of collective progress

### Moodboard 3 — Zimasa Go / Members
**Keyword**: easy, social, active
Visual direction: individuals or pairs; movement; mobile-first feel; spontaneous; lighter and warmer; app screens prominent; share cards; leaderboard moments

## 8. Legal & Rights Checklist

Before any image is placed in the repository:

- [ ] Image has a commercial use licence (not editorial-only)
- [ ] If people are identifiable: model release is in place or image is from a rights-managed stock library that includes model releases
- [ ] If showing a product screen: all visible data is mocked — no real names, emails, health events, or org names
- [ ] If using partner or provider imagery: explicit written permission obtained
- [ ] If using team / founder photos: explicit approval from all people shown
- [ ] Image is not AI-generated without explicit sign-off from Founder (reputational risk if detectable)
