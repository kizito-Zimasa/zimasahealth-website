## Section 2 — The Problem

**Eyebrow**: The Challenge

**Headline**: Wellness Benefits That Nobody Uses

**Body**: Most employer and payer wellness programmes suffer the same problem: low activation, no verification, and no measurable ROI. Members register once, use the platform for two weeks, and disappear. You're left with utilisation reports that measure logins, not health outcomes.

**3 problem tiles**:

**Problem 1 — Low Activation**  
Icon: `trending-down`  
Industry average activation rates sit below 20%. Most members never progress past onboarding.

**Problem 2 — No Verification**  
Icon: `x-circle`  
Self-reported activity data is unreliable. You cannot risk-adjust, report to boards, or design renewal strategy on unverified numbers.

**Problem 3 — No Behaviour Change**  
Icon: `repeat`  
Gym discounts and app downloads do not change behaviour. Without habit design, social accountability, and the right incentive structure, nothing sticks past Week 2.

## Section 5 — Reporting & Verification

**Eyebrow**: The Data Layer

**Headline**: Active Days, Not App Sessions

**Body**: ZHEP reports engagement using Active Days — the number of days in a period where a member completed at least one verified health activity. It is a hard metric. Every activity that counts toward an Active Day is verified at the point of service.

**Reporting cadence**:

| Report | Timing | Audience |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Member dashboard | Real-time | Member |
| Engagement snapshot | Weekly | Payer / Employer |
| Cohort dashboard | Monthly | Payer / Employer |
| Programme review | 30/60/90-day | CMO / HR Director |
| Renewal readiness | 90-day | CMO / CRO |

**PHI note**: Zimasa stores verification of engagement events, not health records or diagnoses. All reporting is at cohort or aggregate level. Individual member data is visible only to the member.
