## Section 2 — What We Collect and Why

**Headline**: Data We Collect

### Account and Identity Data
- Name, email address, phone number
- Organisation name (for payer and employer accounts)
- Role (for payer/employer admin accounts)

**Why**: To create and manage your account, and to communicate with you about your programme.

### Engagement Event Data
- Verified activity completions (gym check-ins, session completions, module completions)
- Active Days count per member
- Track selection and programme participation

**Why**: To deliver the engagement programme, calculate Active Days, and generate reporting for payers and employers.

### Usage Data
- Pages visited, features used, time spent
- Device type, browser, operating system
- IP address (anonymised after 30 days)

**Why**: To improve the platform and diagnose technical issues.

### Communication Data
- Email and SMS interactions related to your programme
- Form submissions (Book Demo, Contact Us)

**Why**: To respond to inquiries and deliver programme communications.

## Section 3 — What We Do Not Collect

**Headline**: Data We Do Not Collect

Zimasa is PHI-avoidant by design. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

- Diagnoses or clinical assessments
- Prescription or medication records
- Lab results or pathology data
- Medical history or health records
- Biometric data (blood pressure, glucose, etc.)

All engagement events verified by Zimasa are behavioural — a member completed an activity. We do not record the clinical context or outcome of that activity.

## Section 5 — Data Sharing

**Headline**: Who We Share Data With

We do not sell or rent your data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

**Payers and Employers**: We share aggregate and cohort-level engagement data with the payer or employer who administers your programme. This data does not include individual member health information — only verified engagement event counts and Active Days.

**Service Providers**: We use third-party service providers who process data on our behalf (hosting, email delivery, analytics). All providers are bound by data processing agreements.

**Legal Requirements**: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.

**Provider Network**: Your name and booking details are shared with the specific healthcare provider when you make a booking. No clinical data is shared beyond what you provide directly to the provider.

## Section 6 — Data Retention

**Headline**: How Long We Keep Data

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|----------------|
| Account data | Duration of account + 2 years |
| Engagement event data | 3 years from event date |
| Communication records | 2 years |
| Usage/analytics data | 13 months (rolling) |
| Form submissions | 2 years |

After retention periods, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

## Section 7 — Your Rights (KDPA)

| Right | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|

| Correction | Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data |

**How to exercise your rights**: Email privacy@zimasahealth.com with your request. We will respond within 21 days.

## Section 8 — Security

**Headline**: How We Protect Your Data

**Technical safeguards**:
- All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
- Data encrypted at rest (AES-256)
- Access controls — role-based, least-privilege
- Regular security reviews and penetration testing

**Organisational safeguards**:
- Staff data protection training
- Data breach response procedure
- Vendor due diligence process
- Incident notification within 72 hours (ODPC)

## Section 9 — Cookies

**Headline**: Cookies and Tracking

We use the following types of cookies:

**Essential cookies**: Required for the platform to function. Cannot be disabled.

**Analytics cookies**: Google Analytics 4 — used to understand how the site is used. Fired only after you provide consent via the cookie banner.

**Preference cookies**: Remember your settings and preferences.

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

We comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 consent requirements — analytics cookies are not set until you accept them.
