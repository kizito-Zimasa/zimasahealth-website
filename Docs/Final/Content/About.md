## Section 2 — Founder Story

**Eyebrow**: Origin

**Headline**: From Processing Claims to Preventing Them

**Body**:

After years working in health insurance operations across East Africa, our founder saw the same pattern repeating: low member engagement with wellness benefits, rising claims costs, and no feedback loop connecting payer investment to member behaviour.

The question was: what if instead of building better claims infrastructure, we built better engagement infrastructure?

That shift — from reactive to preventive, from transactional to behavioural — is the foundation of Zimasa.

We are building the Health Engagement OS for African health markets: a platform that connects payers and employers to a vetted provider network, runs structured health programmes, and generates the verified engagement data that risk-conscious buyers need.

**Pull quote**: "The real problem wasn't processing claims faster. It was preventing them."

## Section 3 — Mission & Values

**Eyebrow**: What We Stand For

**Headline**: Our Mission

**Mission statement**: To drive measurable health activation across African member populations — through verified engagement, behaviour design, and a curated provider network.

**3 values**:

**Verification Over Vanity**  
Icon: `check-circle`  
We only report what we can verify. Active Days are earned through confirmed activity — not app logins, self-declarations, or estimates. If we can't verify it, it doesn't count.

**Africa-First, Always**  
Icon: `map-pin`  
Every product decision is made for East African market realities: MPESA-native rewards, local provider economics, NHIF-adjacent programme design, and infrastructure that works in variable connectivity.

**PHI-Avoidant by Design**  
Icon: `lock`  
We built the platform to deliver engagement outcomes without touching protected health information. Payers and employers get the data they need; member clinical data stays where it belongs.

## Section 6 — Compliance & Credentials

**Eyebrow**: Standards & Compliance

**Headline**: Built to Regulatory Standard

**Compliance points**:

**Kenya Data Protection Act 2019**  
Icon: `shield`  
Zimasa's data handling practices comply with the KDPA. Member data collection is consent-based. A full privacy notice and data processing disclosure is available in the Privacy Centre.

**PHI-Avoidant Architecture**  
Icon: `lock`  
The platform does not collect, store, or transmit protected health information. All engagement data is event-based and aggregate-reportable.

**Provider Vetting Standards**  
Icon: `badge-check`  
All network providers are vetted against professional registration, insurance, and quality standards before activation. Ongoing performance monitoring applies.
