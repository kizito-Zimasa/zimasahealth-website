## Section 3 — The 30/60/90 Dashboard Suite

**Eyebrow**: Reporting

**Headline**: Three Dashboards. Three Decisions.

**Body**:
Most wellness dashboards report what members did. ZPS dashboards are designed around what payer teams decide next. Each stage of the programme cycle has a corresponding dashboard built for that stage's stakeholders.

**Dashboard layout** (tabbed or stacked on mobile, three-panel on desktop):

---

### Dashboard 1 — 30-Day Activation
**Audience**: Head of Member Experience / CMO  
**Decision**: Is the programme launching? Which cohorts need activation support?

**Headline metric**: Activation Rate — members who completed at least one verified activity in the first 30 days

**Key metrics**:
- Total members enrolled vs. activated
- Activation rate by cohort, age band, and geography
- Track distribution (MOVE / NOURISH / CARE / LEARN)
- Top 10 most active providers in network
- Members with zero activity (flagged for re-engagement)

---

### Dashboard 2 — 60-Day Engagement Depth
**Audience**: CMO / Risk Manager  
**Decision**: Who is forming habits? Where is the programme losing members?

**Headline metric**: Active Days per member per month — by cohort

**Key metrics**:
- Active Days per member per month (by cohort and segment)
- Track retention curves — who started MOVE and is still active?
- Provider utilisation rate — booked vs. attended
- Challenge participation and completion rates
- Drop-off analysis by cohort (with last-activity date)

---

### Dashboard 3 — 90-Day Renewal Readiness
**Audience**: CMO / CRO / Actuary  
**Decision**: What does this programme demonstrate for the renewal conversation?

**Headline metric**: Cohort-level Active Days trajectory — engagement trend from Day 1 to Day 90

**Key metrics**:
- Cohort-level Active Days trajectory
- High-engagement vs. low-engagement segment split
- CARE track completions — preventive interventions verified
- Engagement programme ROI summary (cost per active member)
- Renewal-ready cohort narrative (exportable for board packs)

## Section 4 — Programme Delivery Model

**Eyebrow**: How It Works

**Headline**: Four Phases. Ninety Days. A Report You Can Use.

**Body**:
Every ZPS engagement is structured as a 90-day programme. There is no undefined run time and no open-ended subscription. Zimasa delivers a programme with defined phases, deliverables at each stage, and a renewal-ready report at the end.

**Phase timeline** (horizontal steps on desktop, stacked on mobile):

**Phase 1 — Programme Design (Weeks 1–2)**  
Icon: `clipboard-list`  
Work with the Zimasa programme team to define your 90-day engagement brief: target cohort, track mix (MOVE / NOURISH / CARE / LEARN), incentive structure, reporting requirements, and success thresholds. Deliverable: signed-off programme brief.

**Phase 2 — Activation (Days 1–30)**  
Icon: `users`  
Members onboard to ZHEP under your brand. The Community Engine runs challenges and accountability structures from Day 1. Zimasa sends your team the 30-day activation dashboard at the end of this phase. Deliverable: 30-day activation dashboard.

**Phase 3 — Engagement Depth (Days 31–60)**  
Icon: `activity`  
Engagement moves from onboarding to habit formation. Zimasa monitors drop-off, runs targeted re-engagement for low-activity cohorts, and delivers the 60-day engagement depth dashboard. Deliverable: 60-day engagement depth dashboard.

**Phase 4 — Renewal Readiness (Days 61–90)**  
Icon: `file-text`  
The final phase generates your renewal-ready evidence pack: cohort trajectory, segment analysis, CARE track interventions, and an ROI summary formatted for actuarial and board audiences. Deliverable: 90-day renewal readiness report.

## Section 6 — PHI-Avoidant Architecture

**Eyebrow**: Data & Compliance

**Headline**: Built Without PHI from the Ground Up

**Body**:
ZPS was designed for the payer compliance environment from the start. The platform verifies participation in health activities without accessing or storing clinical information. Your legal, compliance, and data protection teams have a clear story to tell.

**What ZPS records**:
- Verified engagement events (gym check-in, dietitian booking completed, education module finished)
- Active Days count per member per period
- Cohort-level aggregate metrics

**What ZPS does not record**:
- Diagnoses, prescriptions, or treatment plans
- Lab results or screening outcomes
- Any clinical notes or health records

**Reporting level**: Cohort and aggregate only. Individual member data is visible only to the member. Your dashboards never show individual employee or member health data.

**Regulatory**: Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 compliant. KDPA Privacy Impact Assessment available on request.
