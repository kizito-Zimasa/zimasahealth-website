## Section 3 — How the Platform Works

**Eyebrow**: The Architecture

**Headline**: One Platform, Configured for Each Buyer

**Diagram / 3-column layout**:

**Column 1 — Payer or Employer**  
Configures programme scope, benefit budget, and reporting cadence. Receives white-label portal and weekly/monthly dashboards.

**Column 2 — ZHEP Platform**  
Routes members to relevant tracks. Runs the Community Engine. Manages the Rewards Engine. Verifies every engagement event.

**Column 3 — Provider Network**  
Verified gyms, dietitians, therapists, labs. Members access via booking flow. Providers receive verified member referrals.
