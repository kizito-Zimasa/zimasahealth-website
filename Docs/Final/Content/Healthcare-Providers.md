## Section 2 — The Provider Opportunity

**Eyebrow**: Why Join

**Headline**: Qualified Referrals. Verified Engagement. Predictable Volume.

**Body**: Most healthcare providers spend money acquiring patients through word-of-mouth, social media, or walk-in traffic. Zimasa offers a different model: structured referrals from employer and payer wellness programmes, with pre-engaged members who have already committed to a health track.

**3 value points**:

**Verified Referrals**  
Icon: `badge-check`  
Members in the Zimasa network arrive through structured health tracks — not cold acquisition. They have already onboarded, selected their health interests, and activated their engagement programme.

**Predictable Volume**  
Icon: `bar-chart-2`  
Payer and employer programmes generate consistent monthly member flow. Providers in high-demand tracks see predictable booking volumes.

**No Referral Fees**  
Icon: `check-circle`  
Zimasa does not charge per-referral fees to providers. Network membership is based on vetting and quality standards. Member payment flows are handled through the platform.

## Section 3 — Provider Types We Accept

**Eyebrow**: Who Qualifies

**Headline**: Four Tracks. Multiple Provider Categories.

**Body**: The Zimasa provider network is structured around the four ZHEP health tracks. We accept verified providers in each category:

**MOVE Track Providers**:
- Accredited gyms and fitness centres
- Group fitness instructors (in-person and live-stream)
- Personal training studios
- Run clubs and sports organisations

**NOURISH Track Providers**:
- Registered dietitians
- Certified nutritionists
- Meal planning services
- Food education practitioners

**CARE Track Providers**:
- Licensed mental health therapists and counsellors
- Registered physiotherapists
- Accredited diagnostic labs (preventive screening packages)
- Specialist referral partners

**LEARN Track Providers**:
- Accredited health education content creators
- Wellness practitioners running evidence-based programmes

## Section 4 — How Verification Works

**Eyebrow**: Quality Standards

**Headline**: Every Provider Is Vetted Before Going Live

**Process steps** (numbered or icon list):

**Step 1 — Application**  
Submit your provider profile, credentials, and service offering through the Zimasa provider portal.

**Step 2 — Credential Verification**  
The Zimasa network team verifies licences, certifications, and relevant accreditations. Turnaround: 5–7 business days.

**Step 3 — Onboarding**  
Approved providers are onboarded to the Zimasa system: QR check-in setup, booking integration, and dashboard access.

**Step 4 — Go Live**  
Your facility or practice becomes discoverable to members in relevant health tracks. Members book through ZHEP, check in on arrival, and their Active Day is recorded.

**Step 5 — Performance Monitoring**  
Zimasa monitors member satisfaction and engagement quality. Providers with sustained low ratings are reviewed.
