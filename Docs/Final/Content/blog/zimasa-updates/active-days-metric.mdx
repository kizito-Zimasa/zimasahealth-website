# Why We Obsess Over Active Days (And Why You Should Too)

## The Metric Problem

Health and wellness programs are drowning in metrics. Enrollment. Participation rate. Claims saved. Surveys completed.

But none of these actually tell you if your program *worked*.

- **Enrollment** is a vanity metric. Signing up isn't engagement.
- **Participation rate** is vague. Does one click count? One minute? One day?
- **Claims savings** is backward-looking and confounded by dozens of variables.
- **Surveys** measure satisfaction, not behavior change.

What's missing? A **real-time, verified measure of engagement that predicts outcomes**.

## Introducing Active Days

Active Days is simple: one day per member where they completed at least one verified health action.

Verified means:
- Gym check-in via partner network
- Steps logged in the app
- Screening booked and attended
- Class completed
- Habit logged

Not self-reported. Not surveyed. **Verified.**

### Why This Matters

Active Days predict:
1. **Retention** — Members with 8+ Active Days/month renew at 5× the rate of single-action members
2. **Health Outcomes** — Consistent engagement correlates with utilization of preventive care
3. **Employer ROI** — Workforce with 8+ Active Days/month shows measurable improvement in sick leave, absenteeism, and retention

### The 90-Day Window

In pilot programs, we measure success in 90 days:
- **Activation** — 50–70% of eligible members achieve at least one Active Day
- **Consistency** — Engaged members average 8–12 Active Days/month
- **Durability** — Members show retention at week 13 (post-program)

These benchmarks come from 18 months of pilot data across employers and payers.

### Beyond the Number

Active Days isn't just a metric. It's a north star. It forces product design around what actually drives engagement:
- Community (squad accountability)
- Visibility (leaderboards)
- Progress (verified outcomes)
- Incentives (rewards that scale)

Everything else — content, messaging, provider partnerships — exists to increase Active Days.
