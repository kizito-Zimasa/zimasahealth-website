# Zimasa is Live: Health Engagement OS for Payers and Employers

## 18 Months of Learning

We started Zimasa with a hypothesis: **health engagement programs fail because they treat engagement as a compliance checkbox, not a community experience.**

We've spent the last 18 months testing this with pilots across Kenya:

- 12 employer programmes (2,500+ employees)
- 4 payer cohorts (8,000+ members)
- 45+ health provider partnerships
- 340,000+ verified Active Days logged

And our hypothesis was right. But we also learned more.

## What We Learned

**1. Community drives engagement.** Squads of 3–5 people, competing via leaderboards, unlock 60%+ activation in 90 days. Compliance drives 5–10%.

**2. Verification matters.** Members who log steps themselves, attend classes in person, or complete screenings stay engaged. Self-reported wellness data drops off at week 3.

**3. Active Days is the right metric.** Enrollment is a vanity metric. Claims savings is backward-looking. Active Days (one day per member with at least one verified health action) predicts retention, outcomes, and ROI.

**4. Payers and employers have different needs.** Payers need risk-targeted cohorts and renewal leverage. Employers need workforce retention and engagement culture. Both need Active Days visibility.

## What We're Building

### ZHEP: The Core Platform
- **Four Tracks**: MOVE (fitness), NOURISH (nutrition), CARE (expert interventions), LEARN (education)
- **Community Engine**: Squads, challenges, leaderboards
- **Rewards Engine**: Partner discounts, incentives, points
- **Verification**: Gym check-ins, app logging, screening bookings

### ZPS: Zimasa Payer Solution
- **Cohort Studio**: AI-assisted program design (condition, demographics, duration)
- **Risk Targeting**: Members by utilization, condition, or engagement gap
- **Active Days Dashboard**: Real-time member engagement for payers
- **Renewal Ready**: Documented engagement for renewal conversations

### Zimasa Go: Free Tier
- Members sign up free
- Access squads, challenges, leaderboards
- Employers can register workforce for free admin dashboard
- Seamless upgrade path to full programme

But our north star stays the same: **verified engagement at scale.**
