# The Activation Problem: Why 80% of Your Wellness Program Sits Unused

## The Hidden Cost of Low Adoption

Your company offers a wellness program. Gym stipend. Mental health app. Nutrition classes. All subsidized.

But you know the dirty secret: **most employees don't use it.**

Maybe 15–20% are active. The rest? They signed up, looked around, and never came back.

The costs are hidden:
- Sunk investment in benefits you're not getting ROI on
- Continued health claims from engaged employees
- Turnover risk (employees cite "no wellness culture" in exit interviews)
- Competitive disadvantage (peers offer "active" wellness programs)

## Why Adoption Fails

Traditional wellness programs fail for three reasons:

**1. No peer pressure.** If your gym membership is optional and private, you're relying on individual motivation. That works for 10–15% of people (the self-starters). Everyone else needs accountability.

**2. No progress visibility.** You can't see who's participating or how they're doing. So engagement feels invisible. You might be the only person at the company working out on Tuesday.

**3. No community.** Wellness feels transactional. You log your activity, the system tracks it, and... then what? No one celebrates the milestone. No one notices.

## The Community Fix

Zimasa's Zimasa Go tier fixes this by adding three elements:

**Squads.** Employees form teams of 3–5 (often existing teams: your department, your floor, your affinity group). One squad challenge per month (log steps together, complete a screening, attend a class). Squad leaderboard so everyone sees progress.

**Challenges.** Company-wide and squad challenges create friendly competition. "Complete 5 workouts in April" or "Log 50,000 steps together." Completion earns points.

**Rewards.** Points unlock partner discounts (gyms, food, services), cash-back, or donations to charity. Real value.

## The Activation Curve

In Zimasa Go pilots with employers:
- **Week 1:** 40% of workforce signs up (social proof + peer invitation)
- **Week 2-4:** 60% participate in squad challenge (peer pressure works)
- **Week 5-12:** 55% maintain active engagement (consistent 8+ Active Days/month)

Active Days per month climb from 2–3 (sporadic) to 8–12 (consistent) in engaged members.

1. **Pilot Zimasa Go** with your most social team or department
2. **Form squads** (3–5 people, ideally natural teams)
