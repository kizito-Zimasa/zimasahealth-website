## The Mismatch

Let's face it: Africa's health and wellness industries are growing faster than a morning jogger after a double espresso. There's not a day that passes without new gyms, clinics, wellness centres, and nutrition practices opening. Ironically, the more players in the field, the more fragmented care seems to become.

It's not uncommon for a health provider to attend to your high blood pressure without any knowledge of your fitness routine. Your nutritionist recommends a meal plan that your physiotherapist knows nothing about. A wellness provider does a great job helping reduce stress — but that data never reaches your doctor.

Should you be diagnosed with a health condition attributed to stress, you have nothing to back up the fact that you've been proactively managing it with a wellness coach. In the end, other contributing factors get thrown out and the doctor's prescription may not be as effective.

Think of it as a puzzle you are trying to build, but each piece is working so hard by itself that no one can see the full picture.

This is where Zimasa comes in.

## What Zimasa Means for Different Providers

### Health Providers

The goal is to go beyond the consultation room — giving doctors, nurses, and clinical teams a digital extension of their clinic. Imagine a platform that keeps you connected to your patients even after they walk out the door.

Instead of waiting for the patient's next check-up, health providers stay one step ahead: tracking preventive care metrics and monitoring adherence to wellness plans.

### Wellness Providers

Gone are the days when wellness coaches, mental health professionals, and lifestyle mentors were treated like the "extra" in someone's health journey. It's time to grab a seat at the table.

The platform is designed to enable wellness providers to reach individuals who value holistic and preventive care — with access to personalised programmes informed by fitness data. Collaboration with nutritionists and doctors helps you understand the client's health through multiple lenses, making your services impactful in the long run.

### Movement and Fitness Providers

Every stretch, every squat, every kilometre counts. But what if you could track the progress and have something to show for it beyond the gym floor?

The Zimasa platform lets you integrate training data with nutrition and clinical care plans, and track measurable progress in real time. Collaboration with doctors and physiotherapists ensures that the "no pain, no gain" mantra meets "measurable results" — so members are not only moving, but moving in the right direction.

### Nutritionists

Nutrition is the missing ingredient in holistic care — the bridge between physical performance and medical advice. The Zimasa engagement platform ensures that the guidance given by a nutritionist is not only good, but transformational.

As a nutritionist, you can connect your dietary plans directly with clinical insights and track how nutrition impacts health metrics. The right meal plan could be the difference between thriving and surviving — and through Zimasa, you will have the data to prove it.
