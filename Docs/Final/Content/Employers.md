## Section 6 — Employer Proof Points

**Eyebrow**: Why It Works

**Headline**: Built on Verified Engagement, Not Self-Reported Data

**3 proof points** (icon + headline + body):

**Point 1 — Active Days Are Verified**  
Icon: `check-circle`  
Every gym check-in, screening, expert session, and nutrition appointment is verified through the Zimasa provider network. Your report is not based on what employees say they did — it is based on what the system confirmed.

**Point 2 — PHI-Avoidant by Design**  
Icon: `lock`  
Zimasa does not collect or store protected health information. Your HR dashboard shows population-level engagement — activation rates, Active Days, track mix — never individual diagnoses, prescriptions, or clinical records.

**Point 3 — No IT Integration Required**  
Icon: `zap`  
Zimasa Go runs on a single invite link. Employees sign up on their own devices. No SSO, no HRIS integration, no IT procurement. The full programme adds optional payroll-linked allowances but never requires system access.
