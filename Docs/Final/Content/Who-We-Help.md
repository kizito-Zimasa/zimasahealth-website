## Section 2 — Persona 1 — Payers & Schemes

**Eyebrow**: Segment 1

**Badge**: Highest Priority

**Headline**: Health Insurers, TPAs & Medical Schemes

**Roles**: CMO · CRO · Risk Manager · Head of Member Experience

**The challenge they face**:  
Payers carry the cost risk of a member population they cannot easily influence. Low member activation means wellness benefits are a cost line, not a risk tool. Renewal conversations happen without engagement evidence.

**What they need from Zimasa**:
- Verified cohort-level engagement data
- A structured 90-day programme they can point to at renewal
- White-label member experience under their scheme brand
- Dashboards CMOs can present to actuaries and boards

**Key metrics**:
- Activation rate by cohort and age band
- Active Days per member per month
- CARE track utilisation (preventive interventions)
- 90-day engagement trajectory

**Pricing model**: PMPM (per member per month) — KES 400–800 base  
*(pricing not quoted on site — direct to demo)*

**CTA**: See Payers Solution → `/insurers-tpas/`

## Section 3 — Persona 2 — Healthcare Providers

**Eyebrow**: Segment 2

**Headline**: Practice Owners, Clinic Leads & Gym Operators

**Roles**: Practice Owner · Clinic Lead · Gym Manager · Dietitian · Therapist

**The challenge they face**:  
Healthcare providers spend resources acquiring patients through word-of-mouth or social media. Quality referrals are expensive and unpredictable. Patients often arrive without context on their health goals.

**What they need from Zimasa**:
- A consistent stream of pre-engaged, verified member referrals
- Simple check-in system that records activity automatically
- Visibility into booking patterns and utilisation
- No acquisition cost — network membership replaces referral spend

**Key metrics**:
- Monthly member visits
- Track-specific booking volume
- Member satisfaction ratings

**CTA**: Join the Network → `/healthcare-providers/`

## Section 4 — Persona 3 — Employers & HR

**Eyebrow**: Segment 3

**Badge**: Phase 2 Priority

**Headline**: HR Directors, CFOs & People Teams

**Roles**: HR Director · CFO · Head of People · Benefits Manager

**The challenge they face**:  
Employers invest in wellness benefits that generate low utilisation and no measurable ROI. Annual health check budgets are spent on services that don't change member behaviour. The board asks for evidence of impact and the HR team has none.

**What they need from Zimasa**:
- A workforce wellness programme with verifiable utilisation data
- Track-mix reporting they can present at board level
- A per-employee-per-month model with predictable cost
- A programme that goes beyond gym subsidies

**Key metrics**:
- Active Days per employee per month
- Track utilisation mix (MOVE / NOURISH / CARE / LEARN)
- Engagement rate vs. enrolled population
- Cost per active member

**Available Now:** Zimasa Go (free tier)  
**Coming Q4 2026:** Full programme with dashboards and SLAs  
**Use Case:** Self-serve free tier, or full programme with dashboards (Phase 2)  
**ROI:** Utilization visibility and employee retention

**Pricing model**: PEPM (per employee per month) — KES 950–5,500  
*(pricing not quoted on site — direct to demo)*

**CTA**: Book a Demo → `/book-demo/`

## Section 5 — The Common Thread

**Eyebrow**: What Every Buyer Shares

**Headline**: Verified Engagement. Measurable in 90 Days.

**Body**: Every buyer type has a different goal — payers want risk visibility, employers want utilisation evidence, providers want qualified referrals. What they share is a need for data they can trust.

Zimasa's verification model means every Active Day is earned, not estimated. Every engagement event is confirmed at the point of service. Every report reflects real behaviour, not app sessions or self-declarations.

**Proof point**: 50–70% activation rate within 90 days. 8–12 Active Days per member per month for engaged members.
