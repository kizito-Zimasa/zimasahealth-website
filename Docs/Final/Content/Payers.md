## Section 2 — The Payer Problem

**Eyebrow**: The Challenge

**Headline**: You're Paying for Wellness Benefits That Don't Move the Needle

**Body**: Most health schemes and TPAs offer wellness benefits — gym discounts, app subscriptions, health checks. The utilisation numbers are low, the data is unverified, and there is no clear line between wellness spend and risk trajectory. Renewal conversations happen without engagement evidence.

**3 problem statements**:

**Problem 1 — Low Member Activation**  
Icon: `trending-down`  
Members register and disappear. Engagement drops below 15% after the first month. You have no visibility into which cohorts are genuinely active.

**Problem 2 — Unverified Data**  
Icon: `alert-triangle`  
Self-reported wellness activity cannot support risk adjustment or board reporting. Without verification, your wellness spend is a cost centre, not a risk tool.

**Problem 3 — No Renewal Leverage**  
Icon: `file-x`  
When renewal negotiations begin, you lack the activation data to justify your engagement investment or demonstrate risk trajectory improvements.

## Section 6 — PHI Policy

**Eyebrow**: Data & Compliance

**Headline**: Built Without PHI from the Ground Up

**Body**: Zimasa does not collect, store, or transmit protected health information. The platform verifies engagement events — a member completed a gym session, booked a dietitian appointment, finished a health education module — without accessing or recording clinical data.

**What Zimasa records**: Verified engagement events and Active Days  
**What Zimasa does not record**: Diagnoses, prescriptions, lab results, or clinical notes  
**Reporting level**: Cohort and aggregate — individual member data is visible only to the member  
**Applicable regulation**: Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 compliant
