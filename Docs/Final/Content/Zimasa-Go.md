## Section 2 — What Is Zimasa Go

**Eyebrow**: Free for Everyone

**Headline**: Your Health Journey Starts Here

**Body**:
Zimasa Go is the free starting point for health engagement. Whether you're joining through your employer or starting on your own, Zimasa Go gives you the tools to track movement, build streaks, connect with a small squad, and see your Active Days grow.

No gym membership required. No employer needed. Just you, your squad, and a goal.

**4 feature tiles** (2×2 grid on desktop):

### Tile 1 — Steps & Activity Tracking
**Icon**: `footprints`  
**Headline**: Track Every Active Day  
**Body**: **Active Days** — Earned when you log steps, join a class, or complete a challenge. One active day = one action logged.

### Tile 2 — Squads
**Icon**: `users`  
**Headline**: Build Your Squad  
**Body**: Form a squad of 3–5 people — friends, family, colleagues. See each other's Active Days, cheer progress, and hold each other to account.

### Tile 3 — Public Challenges & Leaderboards
**Icon**: `trophy`  
**Headline**: Compete and Celebrate  
**Body**: Join public monthly challenges. Climb the leaderboard. Zimasa Go runs community-wide challenges across categories — steps, consistency streaks, nutrition habits, and more.

### Tile 4 — Share Cards
**Icon**: `share-2`  
**Headline**: Share Your Progress  
**Body**: Generate a share card when you hit milestones — weekly streak, squad challenge win, personal best. Designed for WhatsApp and Instagram Stories.

## Section 3 — How It Works

**Eyebrow**: Get Started in Minutes

**Headline**: Three Steps to Your First Active Day

**Steps** (horizontal on desktop, stacked on mobile):

**Step 1 — Sign Up Free**  
Icon: `user-plus`  
Go to Get Started and choose the member path. Enter your name and email. No credit card, no subscription.

**Step 2 — Build Your Squad**  
Icon: `users`  
Invite 2–4 people to join your squad. Share your squad link on WhatsApp. Everyone tracks together from day one.

**Step 3 — Start Earning Active Days**  
Icon: `activity`  
Log your first activity. Track your streak. Join a public challenge. See your Active Days count grow on the leaderboard.

## Section 4 — What Zimasa Go Includes

**Eyebrow**: Free Forever

**Headline**: Everything You Need to Start

Two-column layout: what's included / what requires a programme (employer or payer):

**Zimasa Go — Always Free**
- Steps and activity tracking
- Squads (3–5 people)
- Public challenges and community leaderboards
- Share cards for milestones
- Personal Active Days streak

**Full Programme** (through your employer or payer):
- Four health tracks: MOVE, NOURISH, CARE, LEARN
- Access to vetted providers — gyms, dietitians, physios, labs
- Private company leagues and custom challenges
- Allowances and rewards for verified engagement
- Employer HR dashboard

**Note**: If your employer or health scheme uses Zimasa, you automatically get access to the full programme. Zimasa Go is your independent starting point if they don't — yet.
