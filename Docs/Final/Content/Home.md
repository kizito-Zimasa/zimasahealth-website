## Section 2A — Active Days Definition

**Headline**: What Is Active Days?

**Body**: Active Days measures verified health engagement — one day per member where they completed at least one action (logged steps, attended a class, completed a screening). This metric matters because it predicts program ROI and member retention.

## Section 5 — How It Works

**Eyebrow**: The Engagement Model

**Headline**: Behaviour Design. Verified Activation. Measurable Outcomes.

**3-step process** (horizontal on desktop, stacked on mobile):

**Step 1 — Enrol**  
Icon: `users`  
Members join via employer or payer portal. They choose their health tracks and set their baseline.

**Step 2 — Engage**  
Icon: `zap`  
The Community Engine runs challenges, streaks, and leaderboards. The Rewards Engine converts engagement into partner perks and wallet credits.

**Step 3 — Verify**  
Icon: `check-circle`  
Every activity is verified — gym check-ins, screenings, expert sessions. We report Active Days, not self-reported data.
