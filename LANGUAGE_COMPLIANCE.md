# Language & Terminology Compliance Guide

## Brand Standards

### Approved Terminology

#### Product Names
- **ZHEP** — Health Engagement OS (use consistently, not "ZHEP Platform")
- **Zimasa Go** — Freemium tier (capital Z, capital G, not "Zimasa GO" or "zimasa-go" in copy)
- **Payers Solution** — For insurers & TPAs (use "Payers Solution" or "Zimasa for Payers")
- **Health Engagement OS** — Full product name (not "Health Engagement Platform" or "engagement system")

#### Measurements & Metrics
- **Active Days** — Verified engagement metric (always capitalized)
- **Verified engagement** — Core messaging (not "self-reported" or "survey-based")
- **90-day programme** — Standard programme length (use British spelling, not "program")
- **Four tracks** — MOVE, NOURISH, CARE, LEARN (always four, use track not "pillar")

#### Audience Terms
- **Payers & Schemes** — Insurance companies & medical schemes (not "health plans")
- **TPAs** — Third-Party Administrators (acceptable abbreviation, use for clarity)
- **Employers** — Corporate wellness buyers (not "organizations" or "companies" in audience context)
- **Members** — Individuals in programmes (not "employees", "users", or "patients")
- **Providers** — Healthcare professionals in network (gyms, dietitians, therapists, labs)
- **Healthcare providers** — Full term when clarity needed

#### Regional Specificity
- **East Africa** — Market scope (includes Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania)
- **Nairobi-based** — Company location (use, not "Kenya-based")
- **African markets** — Broader market context (not "emerging markets" or "developing markets")

#### Compliance & Privacy
- **PHI-avoidant** — Architecture approach (protected health information, always hyphenated)
- **Kenya Data Protection Act 2019** — Compliance standard (use full name on first reference)
- **NHIF** — National Hospital Insurance Fund (acceptable abbreviation)
- **Privacy-first** — Design philosophy (use "privacy-first" not "privacy-focused")

### Deprecated & Avoid

#### Old Product Names
- ❌ "Claims processing" — outdated, don't use
- ❌ "Health Insurance Platform" — too generic
- ❌ "Member engagement app" — too reductive
- ❌ "Wellness programme software" — vague

#### Outdated Terminology
- ❌ "User" — use "member" or "provider" instead
- ❌ "Patient" — use "member" or "individual" instead
- ❌ "Employee" — use "member" in health context
- ❌ "Enroll in a programme" — use "activate" or "join"
- ❌ "Compliance software" — use "engagement infrastructure"

#### Regional Sensitivity
- ❌ "Emerging market" — use "African markets" or "East Africa"
- ❌ "Developing country" — use specific country names
- ❌ "Underdeveloped" — avoid; use "growing" or specific context
- ❌ "Third world" — outdated terminology

#### Vague Business Terms
- ❌ "Platform solution" — redundant; use "platform" or "solution"
- ❌ "Engagement software" — too generic; use "Health Engagement OS"
- ❌ "Wellness app" — misleading; ZHEP is more than an app
- ❌ "Health tech" — too broad; use "health engagement technology"

#### Weak Messaging
- ❌ "Helps members..." — show impact, not help
- ❌ "May improve..." — use "drives" or "increases" for verified outcomes
- ❌ "Hopes to..." — avoid hedging language
- ❌ "Tries to..." — sound confident, not tentative

## Spelling Standards

### British English (Preferred)
- ✓ Programme (not "program")
- ✓ Behaviour (not "behavior")
- ✓ Centre (not "center") — "Privacy Centre", "Digital Centre"
- ✓ Recognised (not "recognized")
- ✓ Utilisation (not "utilization")
- ✓ Organisation (not "organization")
- ✓ Whilst (not "while") — in formal context
- ✓ Colour (not "color")

### Acceptable Variants
- "Africa-First, Always" — uses American convention for clarity/branding
- "Nairobi-based" — hyphenated for adjective use
- "90-day" — hyphenated when modifying noun

## Tone & Voice Standards

### What We Sound Like
- **Confident** — We know what we've built
- **Specific** — Use data, not generalities
- **Direct** — "Active Days are earned" not "can be earned"
- **Evidence-based** — Reference pilot data, benchmarks
- **Regional-first** — "Built for East Africa" before "Built for global scale"

### What We Don't Sound Like
- Jargony — Avoid buzzwords without definition
- Apologetic — Don't hedge ("hopes to", "may help")
- Corporate — Avoid generic business speak
- Patronizing — Respect audience intelligence
