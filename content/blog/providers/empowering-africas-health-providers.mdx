Health is the greatest wealth you can possess. I'm sure this is not the first time you are coming across this statement, but let's pause and think about the realities surrounding the efforts we actually put in to maintain good health. Most times, it seems like an impossible maze to solve — but what if it were as simple as being a little more invested and engaged in how you balance your health, wellness and fitness, all in one place?

Sounds complicated? Impossible, maybe? I get it. Half the time we are left wondering who the captain of this healthcare system is — the doctor, the nutritionist, the wellness coach or the fitness trainer. How all these services can be coordinated in one place for your benefit is the question I hope to answer by the end of this article, so stick around!

The truth is, our health ecosystem has long been a collection of brilliant islands and nothing close to a connected continent. It is this very reality that has fuelled the existence of Zimasa. At Zimasa, we envision a health system where people are not only encouraged to actively manage their health but also take part in preventive care through a personalised, accessible health engagement platform.

## The Mismatch

Let's face it: the health and wellness industry is growing faster than a morning jogger after a double espresso. Hardly a day passes without a new gym, clinic, wellness centre or nutrition practice opening its doors. Ironically, the more players there are in the field, the more fragmented care seems to become.

It's not uncommon for a doctor to attend to your high blood pressure without any insight into your fitness routine, or for your nutritionist to recommend a meal plan that your physiotherapist knows nothing about. A wellness provider may do a great job of helping you manage stress, but that work rarely reaches your doctor — so the people looking after you each see only part of the picture.

Sound familiar? I bet it does. Think of it as a puzzle where each piece is working so hard by itself that nobody can see the full picture. This is where Zimasa comes in.

## One Platform, One Purpose

Zimasa is a health engagement platform that connects wellness experts, healthcare providers and the main character of the story: you. The aim is to turn isolated efforts into one connected health engagement network. That way, you don't have to wait until you are sick to engage with your health — you are supported to take preventive steps, one at a time.

We are here to make health engagement measurable, understandable and actionable. Think of it as a new health neighbourhood, where the doctor's office is right next to the gym, the yoga studio and the nutrition hub. Through Zimasa, providers can connect around agreed care pathways, so the support you receive is proactive rather than reactive. Everybody wins: you know what to do next, and professionals can see that the next step happened.

## What Zimasa Means for Different Providers

### Health providers

The goal here is to go beyond the consultation room by giving doctors, nurses and clinical teams a way to keep members on agreed care pathways between visits. With ZCare — care continuity on ZHEP — your team can set up reminders, bookings and follow-through sessions, and see programme progress with the member's consent. ZCare supports your clinical judgement; it does not replace it, and it does not diagnose, treat or prescribe.

### Wellness providers

Gone are the days when wellness coaches, mental health professionals and lifestyle mentors were treated as the "extra" in someone's health journey. It's time you had a seat at the table. Zimasa helps wellness providers reach people who value holistic and preventive care, and deliver programmes as part of a wider, coordinated plan.

### Movement and fitness providers

Every stretch, every squat, every kilometre counts — but what if you had something to show for it beyond the gym floor? Through Zimasa, sessions members complete with you count towards their verified Active Days, and fit alongside the other steps in their programme, so they are not only moving, but moving in the right direction.

### Nutritionists

Nutrition is often the missing ingredient in holistic care — the bridge between physical activity and the care plan a member has agreed with their doctor. Through Zimasa, your guidance becomes part of the member's agreed programme rather than a separate leaflet, and you can see whether follow-up sessions happen.

## Bottom Line

Zimasa is building more than a platform: we are building a network that values collaboration over competition among health professionals. Our goal is for people to understand, rather than guess, what to do next for their health. We envision a healthier Kenya where every provider — from clinic to gym, from nutrition to yoga — plays a part in holistic health.

This journey begins with you and me. So the question I'm really asking today is: are you ready to join a network that values connection over competition, follow-through over one-off transactions, and prevention over late care?

If yes, welcome to Zimasa — where connected care shows up, one provider at a time.
