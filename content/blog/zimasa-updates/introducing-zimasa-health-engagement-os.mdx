## The Problem Isn't Coverage. It's Engagement.

If you map today's healthcare ecosystem honestly, you'll notice something striking.

- Payers finance care but have limited ability to influence behaviour before risk becomes a claim
- Providers deliver care episodically, with little continuity outside visits
- Employers sponsor benefits and wellness programmes but struggle to activate them at scale
- Members only interact with the system when something goes wrong

Everyone is doing their job.

No one is orchestrating the whole.

What's missing is not another product. It's an operating layer — a system that coordinates engagement across stakeholders and turns benefits into action.

**That is the problem Zimasa exists to solve.**

## What Zimasa Does: The Engagement Operating Layer

Zimasa provides an engagement operating layer for wellbeing. We combine four elements that typically sit in silos:

**Programme design** — structuring wellbeing and preventive initiatives as coherent journeys rather than isolated activities.

**Digital engagement** — nudges, reminders, and journeys delivered through accessible channels like SMS and WhatsApp.

**Curated service access** — connecting members to relevant wellness and preventive services where included.

**Reporting and visibility** — showing what is being activated, used, and completed.

We start lean — through a First Customer Cohort and engagement reporting — and scale into deeper workflows as rollouts expand. This approach keeps implementation focused and allows organisations to grow engagement deliberately rather than all at once.

## What "Health Engagement" Means in Practice

At Zimasa, engagement is not a vague aspiration. It is a measurable funnel that can be governed, improved, and reported.

We define engagement across five stages:

**Activation** — Members join, consent, complete onboarding, and set initial goals.

**Participation** — Members take regular health actions and build Active Days. An Active Day is a calendar day on which a member completes at least one health action.

**Conversion** — Members take up available services where included, such as screenings or consultations.

**Sustained Behaviour** — Members remain consistent month over month, not just during campaigns.

**Follow-through** — Members take the agreed next step after a screening or consultation, reported in aggregate as leading indicators at 30, 60 and 90 days.

This framework allows organisations to move beyond counting attendance or downloads — and instead manage wellbeing like a performance system.
