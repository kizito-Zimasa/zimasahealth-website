# About Zimasa

## Our Mission

We believe health engagement should be simple, verified, and community-driven. Zimasa is the OS that makes it possible.

## Our Story

Founded in Kenya, Zimasa started with a simple insight: traditional wellness programmes don't work because they treat engagement as a compliance checkbox. Real engagement comes from community, behavior design, and verified outcomes.

We've built Zimasa on three principles:
1. **Community First** — Peer accountability beats compliance mandates
2. **Outcomes Verified** — We measure Active Days, not just sign-ups
3. **Service Factory** — We run the programmes; payers and employers own the data

## Our Team

Built by health, tech, and behavior change experts across Kenya and East Africa.
