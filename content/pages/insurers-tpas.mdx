# Health Engagement for Payers

## Risk Visibility. Renewal Leverage. Engagement ROI.

Zimasa Payer Solution (ZPS) brings verified member engagement to your renewal conversations. Payers run cohort programmes using our four Tracks — MOVE, NOURISH, CARE, LEARN — and track Active Days per eligible member per month.

### Outcomes That Matter
- **Active Days**: 90-day engagement rate 50–70% of eligible members
- **Cohort Programmes**: 90-day programmes targeting risk milestones or utilization gaps
- **Renewal Leverage**: Demonstrated member engagement for renewal conversations

### How It Works
1. **Program Studio** — AI-assisted program design (condition, demographics, duration)
2. **Community Engine** — Challenges, teams, leaderboards, verified engagement
3. **Verification Engine** — Gym check-ins, app logging, biometric integrations
