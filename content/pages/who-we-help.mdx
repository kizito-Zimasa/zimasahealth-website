# Who We Help

## Three Buyer Types

### Payers (Insurers, TPAs, Health Schemes)
**Goal:** Risk visibility and renewal leverage
**Use Case:** Cohort programmes targeting high-utilizers or chronic conditions
**ROI:** Engagement data for renewal conversations

[Explore ZPS](/solutions/zps/)

### Employers (HR/CFO)
**Goal:** Workforce wellness adoption
**Use Case:** Self-serve free tier or full programme with dashboards
**ROI:** Utilization visibility and employee retention

[Explore Zimasa Go or request demo](/book-demo/)

### Members (Individuals)
**Goal:** Community-driven wellness
**Use Case:** Free or subsidised engagement via employer or payer programme
**ROI:** Accountability, motivation, rewards
