# Privacy Centre

## Your Data. Your Control.

Zimasa is built on a privacy-first principle: we store verification of engagement (steps logged, session attended) but never store PHI (protected health information).

### KDPA Compliance
Zimasa complies with Kenya Data Protection Act 2019. Cookie consent is managed via CookieYes.

- Regular security audits
