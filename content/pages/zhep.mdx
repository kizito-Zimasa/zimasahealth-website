# ZHEP: The Core Platform

## Four Tracks. Two Engines. Unlimited Potential.

ZHEP is the foundational engagement platform. Members access four engagement Tracks. Community and Rewards Engines drive adoption and retention.

### The Four Tracks

**MOVE** — Gyms, classes, run clubs, steps
- Partner with fitness facilities
- Log workouts and track activity
- Earn rewards for consistency

**NOURISH** — Nutrition habits, meal plans, food education
- Personalized nutrition guidance
- Meal planning tools
- Partner discounts at healthy food retailers

**CARE** — Expert interventions
- Therapy and counseling
- Dietitian consultations
- Physio and sports medicine
- Labs and screenings

**LEARN** — Education and prevention
- Guided habit pathways
- Condition-specific education
- Preventive screening resources

### The Two Engines

**Community Engine** — Challenges, teams, leaderboards
- Squad accountability
- Weekly and monthly challenges
- Public and private leaderboards
- Badges and milestones

**Rewards Engine** — Incentives and partner discounts
- Points-based rewards
- Partner discounts (gyms, food, services)
- Wallet system
- Gamification

### Built on Outcomes
- Active Days per member per month
- Utilization rates by track
- Activation curves (90-day programmes)
- Risk milestone achievement
