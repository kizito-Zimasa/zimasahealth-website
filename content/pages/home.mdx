# Welcome to Zimasa

## The Health Engagement OS for Payers & Employers

Zimasa is a service factory that runs wellness engagement programmes. We use behavior design, verified outcomes, and community to drive Active Days — the metric that matters for payer and employer ROI.

**Four Tracks. Two Engines. One platform.**

- **MOVE** — Gyms, classes, run clubs, steps
- **NOURISH** — Nutrition habits, meal plans, food education
- **CARE** — Expert interventions: therapy, dietitian, physio, labs/screenings
- **LEARN** — Education and guided prevention/habit pathways

**Community Engine** brings members together via challenges, teams, and leaderboards. **Rewards Engine** incentivizes sustained engagement.
