# Our Solutions

## Choose Your Platform

Zimasa offers two integrated solutions: ZHEP (the core engagement platform) and ZPS (payer-specific programmes).

### ZHEP — Zimasa Health Engagement Platform
The foundational platform powering all engagement. Four Tracks (MOVE, NOURISH, CARE, LEARN). Two Engines (Community, Rewards). Used by employers, payers, and individual members.

### ZPS — Zimasa Payer Solution
Engagement programmes designed for payer risk management. Cohort programmes, active member targeting, and renewal leverage.
