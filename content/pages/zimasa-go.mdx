# Zimasa Go

## Health Engagement, Free to Start

Zimasa Go is the free way to join the wellness community. Track your steps, join squads (3–5 people), complete challenges, earn rewards, and see your impact on leaderboards.

### What's Included
- **Active Days** — Earned when you log steps, join a class, or complete a challenge. One active day = one action logged.
- **Squads** — Form 3–5 person teams for accountability
- **Public Challenges** — Weekly and monthly challenges with prizes
- **Leaderboards** — See your squad and community progress
- **Share Cards** — Celebrate milestones

### What's Not Included (Requires Paid Programme)
- Employer admin dashboard
- Marketplace provider access (gym discounts)
- Allowances or subsidies
- SLAs

### Employer-Lite Dashboard
Register your organisation and give your whole workforce a free employer-lite dashboard. See:
- **Activation Rate** — % of workforce with at least one Active Day
- **Active Days/Employee** — Average per your workforce
- **Track Mix** — % engagement by MOVE, NOURISH, CARE, LEARN
- **Challenge Participation** — Members in active challenges
- **Weekly Reports** — Automatic digest to your inbox

Full reports and CSV export available in paid programmes.

### How It Works
1. Sign up free at [Get Started](/get-started/)
2. Create or join a squad
3. Log activity, complete challenges, earn rewards
4. Tell your employer — they can unlock premium features
