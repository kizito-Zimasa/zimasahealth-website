# ZPS: Zimasa Payer Solution

## Engagement-Driven Risk Management

ZPS brings verified member engagement to your payer strategy. Run cohort programmes, track Active Days, and improve renewal conversations.

### Use Cases
- **Risk Management** — Target members by condition or utilization gap
- **Renewal Leverage** — Demonstrate member engagement to renewing employers
- **Utilization Optimization** — Drive preventive care and screenings
- **Cost Avoidance** — Reduce high-cost utilization through early intervention

### How ZPS Works

**Program Studio** — AI-assisted program design
- Select condition, member cohort, duration
- Choose engagement tracks (MOVE, NOURISH, CARE, LEARN)
- Deploy in 48 hours

**Community Engine** — Challenges and accountability
- Squad-based challenges
- Public and private leaderboards
- Peer accountability

**Verification Engine** — Proven outcomes
- Gym check-ins via partner network
- App logging and biometrics
- Screening completion verification

### Metrics That Matter
- **Active Days**: Engagement rate per eligible member per month
- **Utilization Mix**: By track and intervention type
- **Risk Milestone Achievement**: Screening completion, habit adoption
- **Renewal Conversations**: Documented engagement for renewal
